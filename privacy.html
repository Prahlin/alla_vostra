Alla Vostra Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Alla Vostra provides ordering and delivery coordination for handcrafted grazing boards. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can contact us.

Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, order details, and purchase history. We use this information to process orders, coordinate delivery, provide customer support, and communicate with you about your order.

Payment Information

Payments are processed by third-party payment providers such as Stripe, Apple Pay, and PayPal. Alla Vostra does not store your full payment card number or bank account information.

How We Use Information

We use collected information for app functionality, including order fulfillment, delivery coordination, payment confirmation, fraud prevention, and customer communication. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the app, process payments, send transactional messages, or fulfill orders. These providers are only given information needed to perform their services.

Data Retention

We keep order and contact information as long as needed for business, legal, tax, accounting, support, and security purposes.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and business recordkeeping requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through our contact page.
